Sivakarthikeyan

Actor

Sivakarthikeyan is a popular Indian actor, playback singer, and producer known for his work in Tamil cinema. Starting his career as a television host and comedian, he quickly rose to fame with his natural charm and relatable performances. With hits like *Varuthapadatha Valibar Sangam*, *Remo*, and *Doctor*, he has become a household name, loved for his versatility and ability to blend humor with emotion. Beyond acting, he has ventured into production under his banner, Sivakarthikeyan Productions, and continues to win hearts with his humility and dedication to his craft.
